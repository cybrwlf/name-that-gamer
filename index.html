Name That Gamer

CybrWlf is building an attendance card for everyone who came through the Philadelphia, South Jersey and Delaware LAN scene: who they were, which parties they turned up at, and the photos to prove it. The faces below are the ones still waiting on a name. This page is the first step, and the finished site gets built out of the answers people leave here.

Recognise someone? Hit the button on their card. You will need a GitHub account to answer.

How this works, and what is public
  1. Everything you file is public. GitHub issues can be read by anyone, with or without an account, and they are indexed by search engines. So the form never asks for an email address, a real name or a Steam ID. Do not put one in the notes box either.
  2. Find a face you recognise. Each card shows the three clearest shots of that person the archive has, and every LAN they turn up at.
  3. Hit Name this gamer. It opens a short form on GitHub with the gamer number already filled in. Leave the title alone, it is how we know which face you mean.
  4. The gamer handle is the only thing we really need. Clan is optional, plenty of people ran solo or you may simply not know.
  5. Not sure? Say so on the form. A guess flagged as a guess is useful. A guess passed off as fact is not.
  6. Every card is a guess by the software too. If two different people ended up on one card, or one person got split across two, tick the box that says so.
